Well Monitor questions.

Does it control my well pump?

No. The Well Monitor observes and explains system behavior. It does not switch the pump.

Does it work if the internet goes down?

Local monitoring continues at the property. Remote check-ins and notifications resume when connectivity returns.

Are firmware updates included?

Yes. Security, reliability, and critical bug-fix updates are included for every owner.

Can I add pressure monitoring later?

Yes. Core is designed to accept the Pressure Upgrade Kit without replacing the controller.
