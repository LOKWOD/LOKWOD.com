QUICK CHECK

If your device appears offline

  1. Confirm power: verify that the monitor status light is on.
  2. Check home Wi-Fi: confirm another device can reach the internet.
  3. Wait two minutes: the monitor reconnects automatically after an interruption.
  4. Still offline? Create a connectivity case with your serial number and approximate disconnect time.
