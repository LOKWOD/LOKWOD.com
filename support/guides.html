LOKWOD GUIDE

Connect and register a device

  1. 1

    Power the device and wait for its local dashboard to load.

  2. 2

    Open Advanced Diagnostics and copy the serial number shown there.

  3. 3

    Sign in to the customer portal, choose Add device, and enter that serial exactly.

  4. 4

    Return to the dashboard and confirm the device reports Online and a recent check-in.

LOKWOD GUIDE

Wi-Fi or offline device

  1. 1

    Confirm the device has power and your router has internet access.

  2. 2

    Wait two minutes; LOKWOD automatically retries after an interruption.

  3. 3

    If your network name or password changed, open WiFi Setup from the local dashboard and reconnect.

  4. 4

    Still offline? Send support your serial number, light status, and approximate disconnect time.

LOKWOD GUIDE

Status lights and alerts

  1. 1

    Normal or green means readings are within configured limits.

  2. 2

    Monitor or amber means a trend changed; open the alert for its plain-English next step.

  3. 3

    Critical or red means prompt attention is recommended.

  4. 4

    Never work on pressurized plumbing or energized equipment unless you are qualified to do so.

LOKWOD GUIDE

Firmware update trouble

  1. 1

    Keep the device powered and connected during the entire update.

  2. 2

    Check that the manifest version is newer than the installed version.

  3. 3

    If progress pauses at 100%, allow up to five minutes for verification and restart.

  4. 4

    If it returns to the old version, do not repeat continuously—record the message and contact support.

LOKWOD GUIDE

Pressure and current calibration

  1. 1

    Compare LOKWOD pressure with a known-good mechanical gauge.

  2. 2

    Use the local Settings page to enter the correction only after pressure is stable.

  3. 3

    Calibrate current only with the pump operating normally.

  4. 4

    Save settings and verify the displayed reading before changing alert thresholds.

LOKWOD GUIDE

Reset or transfer ownership

  1. 1

    Do not factory-reset a working device unless support recommends it.

  2. 2

    For a property sale or ownership transfer, contact LOKWOD with the serial number.

  3. 3

    We verify the request before releasing the device from the previous account.

  4. 4

    Wi-Fi credentials can be reset locally without deleting historical account data.
