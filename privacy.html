Privacy

A small site should have a small data footprint.

This page describes information handled by LOKWOD.com. Device and prototype data practices may be documented separately as products develop.

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Website analytics

LOKWOD.com uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and site performance. Cloudflare may process technical request information according to its own terms and policies.

Information you provide

This website currently does not offer user accounts, checkout, or a hosted contact form. Following a link to GitHub or another third-party service moves the interaction to that service and its policies.

Cookies and advertising

LOKWOD.com does not currently operate an advertising network, behavioral advertising profile, or direct-sale checkout. Site functionality may use ordinary browser storage if future interactive features require it; this policy will be updated when that happens.

Device data

LOKWOD development devices are designed around local operation. Specific connectivity, telemetry, update, and retention behavior depends on the product and firmware version. Product documentation should be reviewed before connecting a device to a network.

Changes

This policy may change as the website and products evolve. The date above will be updated when material changes are published.
